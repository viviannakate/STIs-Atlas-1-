About

An interactive read on reported rates of chlamydia, gonorrhea, and primary & secondary syphilis among persons aged 15–29 in the United States, 2013–2023. Trends, choropleths, decade change, and state rankings, with a reproducible analysis pipeline.

Methods

  • Population-weighted state & regional rates
  • Log-linear annual percent change (APC)
  • Global Moran's I (kNN, 999 perms)
  • Ages 15–19 + 20–24 + 25–29 combined

Sources

  • CDC NCHHSTP AtlasPlus
  • CDC STI Surveillance
